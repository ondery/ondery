Who is Önder Yıldırım?

Önder Yıldırım (Necmettin Önder Yıldırım) is a licensed attorney at the Istanbul Bar and a full-stack software architect and AI engineer based in Istanbul, Turkey. He has worked in software for more than 20 years and has practiced law since 2006.

Where does Önder Yıldırım practice law?

He practices at Yıldırım & Yurdaöl Law Office in Istanbul, where he has been an attorney since 2006. His practice includes commercial law, intellectual property, transport and logistics, labor law, enforcement and bankruptcy, family law, and corporate advisory.

What is Bluetree?

Bluetree, formerly LawToolsAI, is a multi-tenant legal AI platform founded and led by Önder Yıldırım. It is built with Next.js, LangGraph, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Where did Önder Yıldırım study?

He earned an LL.B. at Eastern Mediterranean University in Cyprus (2000–2004) and an associate degree from the Software Department at Istanbul University (2023–2025), as a High Honor Student (Onur Öğrencisi). He also graduated from Ankara Tevfik Fikret High School.

Which languages does Önder Yıldırım use?

He works in Turkish, English, and French. His software work uses TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, and PHP.
